The Scenario

The Case: AI-Based Personalized Learning Assistant

A tech company has proposed an AI-powered learning assistant to the college, which is actively considering the tool across all departments.

The AI is designed to:

  • 📈 Track student progress
  • 🎯 Personalize educational content
  • 🛎️ Send early-warning alerts to faculty when a student is at risk of falling behind

The tension: Students and faculty worry about data privacy, AI over-reliance, and bias — yet the tool promises major learning benefits.

The Method

What are the Six Thinking Hats?

A structured technique by Edward de Bono that lets a group focus parallel thinking on one aspect at a time — cutting out snap decisions and ineffective behaviour.

White · Facts
Red · Feelings
Black · Caution
Yellow · Value
Green · Creativity
Blue · Process

White Hat — Facts & Information

Objective thinking

Attributes of this hat

  • Focuses on facts, data & information
  • Neutral & objective — no opinions or judgments
  • Asks “What do we actually know?”
  • Flags what information is still missing

Applied to the case

  • The AI tracks progress, personalizes content, alerts faculty
  • College is considering rollout across all departments
  • Missing: encryption protocols, storage location, whether the company retains access
  • Needed: exact “at-risk” metrics + case studies

Red Hat — Feelings & Intuition

Emotional thinking

Attributes of this hat

  • Focuses on emotions, intuition & gut reactions
  • No burden of logical justification
  • Gives a voice to feelings and hunches
  • Asks “How do we feel about this?”

Applied to the case

  • An algorithm silently tracking every move feels intrusive
  • Fear of being unfairly judged or profiled by a machine
  • Drawn to convenience, but uncomfortable with privacy loss
  • Gut reaction: pressure, frustration, protective instinct toward peers

Black Hat — Caution & Risks

Critical thinking

Attributes of this hat

  • Focuses on risks, dangers & downside
  • Critical risk management & caution
  • Spots loopholes and potential obstacles
  • Asks “What could go wrong?”

Applied to the case

  • Data breach could expose sensitive academic & personal records
  • Over-reliance on spoon-fed content may stunt independent critical thinking
  • Hard to build airtight privacy infrastructure — adoption fails without trust
  • Rushed rollout risks unauthorized use of private data

Yellow Hat — Benefits & Value

Optimistic thinking

Attributes of this hat

  • Focuses on positives, advantages & added value
  • Deliberate optimism and constructive outlook
  • Explores the potential upside
  • Asks “How does this help?”

Applied to the case

  • Improves learning efficiency & overall academic performance
  • Personalized content tailored to each student's pace
  • Transforms delivery of educational support
  • Every student gets a highly individualized learning experience

Green Hat — Creativity & Alternatives

Creative thinking

Attributes of this hat

  • Focuses on new ideas & fresh perspectives
  • Generates creative alternatives
  • Moves beyond “yes or no”
  • Asks “What else could we do?”

Applied to the case

  • Pilot in one tech-literate department first
  • AI notifies at-risk students + one-click voluntary mentoring session
  • Shift perspective: from surveillance to student empowerment
  • Alternative: students input goals manually, no auto-monitoring

Blue Hat — Process & Control

Meta-cognition

Attributes of this hat

  • Controls the flow of thinking
  • Sets the agenda & order of hats
  • Focuses on decision-making process
  • Summarizes conclusions

Applied to the case

  • Agenda: evaluate the AI assistant proposal
  • Order: White → Red → Black → Yellow → Green → Blue
  • Conclusion: immediate college-wide rollout is too risky without safeguards
  • Next step: compile analysis into a formal proposal

The Blue Hat Conclusion

“An immediate, college-wide implementation is too risky without proper safeguards.”

🛡️ Recommended path

Pilot the AI assistant in a single tech-literate department before any broader rollout.

🧭 Student agency

Keep mentoring voluntary via a one-click opt-in — never automatic surveillance.

📄 Next step

Compile this structured analysis into a formal proposal for the college administration.
